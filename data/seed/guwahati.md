# Guwahati - Gateway to Northeast India

## Overview
Guwahati is the largest city in Assam and serves as the gateway to Northeast India. Located on the banks of the Brahmaputra River, it's a blend of ancient temples, modern infrastructure, and natural beauty. The city is a major commercial and educational hub.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Winter**: October to March (10°C - 25°C)
- **Summer**: April to June (hot and humid)
- **Monsoon**: July to September (heavy rainfall)

## How to Reach
- **By Air**: Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (well-connected)
- **By Train**: Guwahati Railway Station (major junction)
- **By Road**: NH27, NH37 connect to major cities

## Major Attractions

### Temples
1. **Kamakhya Temple**: Famous Shakti Peeth, hilltop temple
   - Entry Fee: ₹50 (general), ₹500 (VIP darshan)
   - Timing: 5:30 AM - 10:00 PM
   - Image: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1582510003544-4d00b7f74220
   - Best Time: Early morning or evening
   - Duration: 2-3 hours

2. **Umananda Temple**: On Peacock Island in Brahmaputra
   - Ferry Cost: ₹20 per person (round trip)
   - Entry Fee: Free
   - Image: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1609137144813-7d9921338f24
   - Duration: 2 hours including ferry

3. **Navagraha Temple**: Temple of nine planets
   - Entry Fee: Free
   - Image: https://via.placeholder.com/400x300?text=Navagraha+Temple
   - Duration: 1 hour

4. **Basistha Temple**: Ancient temple with natural surroundings
   - Entry Fee: Free
   - Image: https://via.placeholder.com/400x300?text=Basistha+Temple
   - Duration: 1-2 hours

### Natural Attractions
- **Brahmaputra River Cruise**: Sunset cruises available
  - Cost: ₹300-₹800 per person
  - Duration: 1-2 hours
  - Image: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1544551763-46a013bb70d5
  - Best Time: Evening (5:00 PM - 7:00 PM)

- **Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary**: Rhino sanctuary (50 km)
  - Entry Fee: ₹50 (Indian), ₹500 (Foreign)
  - Jeep Safari: ₹2,000-₹3,000
  - Image: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1564760055775-d63b17a55c44
  - Duration: Half day

- **Deepor Beel**: Bird watching spot
  - Entry Fee: ₹20
  - Boat Ride: ₹100-₹200
  - Image: https://via.placeholder.com/400x300?text=Deepor+Beel
  - Best Time: Early morning

- **Assam State Zoo**: Diverse wildlife
  - Entry Fee: ₹40 (adults), ₹20 (children)
  - Image: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1564349683136-77e08dba1ef7
  - Duration: 2-3 hours

## Travel Tips
- Book river cruises in advance during peak season
- Dress modestly when visiting temples
- Try local street food but choose clean vendors
- Bargain at local markets
- Keep small change for auto-rickshaws
- Best time for photography: Early morning and evening
