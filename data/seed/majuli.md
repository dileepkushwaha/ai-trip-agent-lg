# Majuli Island

## Overview
Majuli is the world's largest river island, located on the Brahmaputra River in Assam. It's a unique cultural landscape known for its Vaishnavite monasteries (Satras), traditional arts, and vibrant tribal culture. The island spans approximately 352 square kilometers.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Winter**: November to March (pleasant weather, 10°C - 25°C)
- **Festival Season**: November (Raas Mahotsav)
- **Avoid**: Monsoon season (June-September) due to flooding
- **Best Photography**: Early morning and sunset

## Tips for Visitors
- Book ferry tickets in advance during peak season
- Carry sufficient cash (limited ATM facilities)
- Rent bicycles for easy exploration (₹100-₹200/day)
- Respect local customs and traditions
- Visit Satras during morning prayers (6:00 AM - 8:00 AM)
- Try homestays for authentic experience
- Pack light (limited porter services)
- Carry mosquito repellent
- Wear comfortable walking shoes
- Learn a few Assamese phrases
- Respect photography restrictions in Satras

## Challenges & Conservation
- **Erosion**: Island is shrinking due to river erosion
- **Flooding**: Annual monsoon floods affect the island
- **Conservation Efforts**: Government and NGO initiatives
- **Sustainable Tourism**: Eco-friendly practices encouraged
