# Kaziranga National Park

## Overview
Kaziranga National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site located in Assam, India. It's famous for hosting the world's largest population of one-horned rhinoceros. The park spans approximately 430 square kilometers and is home to diverse wildlife including tigers, elephants, wild water buffalo, and swamp deer.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season**: November to April
- **Park Closure**: May to October (monsoon season)
- **Best Wildlife Viewing**: February to March
- **Temperature**: 10°C - 25°C (winter)

## How to Reach
- **By Air**: Nearest airport is Jorhat (97 km) or Guwahati (217 km)
  - Taxi from Jorhat: ₹2,500-₹3,500
  - Taxi from Guwahati: ₹4,500-₹6,000
- **By Train**: Nearest railway station is Furkating (75 km)
  - Taxi from station: ₹2,000-₹2,800
- **By Road**: Well-connected by road from Guwahati (4-5 hours drive)
  - Bus: ₹300-₹600
  - Private car: ₹4,000-₹5,500

## Tips for Visitors
- Book safaris 2-3 days in advance during peak season
- Wear neutral-colored clothing (khaki, olive, brown)
- Carry binoculars for better wildlife viewing (₹200-₹500 rental)
- Follow park rules strictly (no littering, no loud noises)
- Hire experienced guides (₹500-₹800)
- Stay quiet during safaris
- Respect wildlife and maintain safe distance
- Carry sunscreen, hat, and water
- Best photography time: Early morning golden hour

## Conservation
Kaziranga is a success story in wildlife conservation, particularly for the one-horned rhinoceros. The park faces challenges from poaching and flooding but continues to thrive with dedicated conservation efforts.

### Conservation Initiatives
- Anti-poaching patrols
- Community involvement programs
- Habitat restoration
- Wildlife corridors
- Research and monitoring
